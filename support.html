Support

Short answers to the questions we hear most. If yours is not here, ask us.

Why is my phone still full after I delete photos?
Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted. They stay there for up to 30 days and keep using space, which is why a phone can still be full after you delete things.
Will it delete my favorites?
Favorites are never deleted. TidyAway leaves them where they are.
What about duplicates?
One copy of every duplicate is always kept.
How is a copy checked?
Every copy is read back and its SHA-256 fingerprint is compared with the original, byte for byte. Copying to OneDrive, the app shows four checks: Copied, byte for byte. Where they are. One brought back and checked. Your OK.
What gets deleted?
TidyAway deletes only what you approve, and then Apple asks you to confirm too. Every copy is checked byte for byte before that.
Can I get a copy back?
Anything TidyAway copied can be brought back.
Where do copies go?
You choose where copies go: a drive plugged into your iPhone or iPad, a folder in Files (iCloud Drive or a home server), Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
Do you see my photos?
No account, no ads and no analytics. Your photos never go to the developer, and scanning happens on the device.
Does it run on iPad?
Yes, TidyAway runs on iPhone and iPad.
What does it cost?
TidyAway is free to download. TidyAway Pro is an in-app purchase. The App Store shows the current prices.
What is coming soon?
Archiving for Live Photos and edited photos, Check-ins (storage reminders on a schedule, and reminders before events in your calendar) and Smart Pro (the best shot with the reason it was chosen, and screenshots read on the device). None of these are in version 1.0.
