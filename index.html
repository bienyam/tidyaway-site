Move it. Prove it. Free space.

TidyAway frees up space on your iPhone. It copies your photos and videos to a place you choose, checks every copy, and deletes only what you approve.

Coming soon on the App Store

For iPhone and iPad.

1

See what takes space

A scan on your iPhone finds large videos, duplicates and more, grouped so you can decide fast.

2

Copy, then check

Copies go to a drive, a folder in Files, Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, and each one is checked against its original.

3

You approve every delete

Nothing is removed until you sign off and Apple asks you to confirm. Copies you made can be brought back any time.
