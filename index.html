Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted. They stay there for up to 30 days and keep using space the whole time. And if a photo was never copied anywhere, deleting it is a gamble.

TidyAway works the other way around. Copy first. Prove it. Then delete.

03The proof

Every copy is checked, byte for byte.
